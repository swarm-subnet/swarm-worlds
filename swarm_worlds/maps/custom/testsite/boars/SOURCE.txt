Source: the wild boars of the Test Field, built for this map.
License: our own work.

Two bodies, each made from its own measures with no outside model: the wild boar (about 1.45 m long and 0.87 m at the
shoulder, in a dark, a grey winter and a rusty summer coat) and its piglet of a few weeks (about 0.42 m long, striped,
or with the stripes fading). Each has bead eyes, hollow ears and forelegs that bend forward at the knee; the boar
carries a mane of bristles along its spine and two small tusks. Body, skeleton, the eleven motions (stand, walk, trot, graze_walk, head_down, graze,
head_up, look, lie_down, lying, get_up), the coats and the heat maps were all made for this map. The motions carry
the goat's names, so one herd's mind moves both: for a boar, graze is rooting in the ground.

boars.json lists each body's coats and clips and which clip may follow which. A skin is one surface of shared
vertices: boar_<name>_poses.npz holds every pose of the body's points (poses), the body point each drawn vertex shows
(source), its place on the coat (uv) and the triangles (faces). The OBJ is the same skin at rest, for any tool that
reads one.
