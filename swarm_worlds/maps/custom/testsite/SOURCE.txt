Source: the Andorra test site (Santa Coloma, Andorra), built from our own drone survey, Govern d'Andorra data
and open data.
License: each source below keeps its original terms, and these credits travel with the assets.

- Field colour and shape, ground near the field, lane walls, roof and tree heights: our own drone survey of the site
  (DJI Matrice 4TD footage, 9 October 2026).
- Govern d'Andorra (IDE Andorra), derived work used with the government's approval, received on 12 November 2026
  and kept with the company's records; no original government file is included: Ortofoto color 25 cm 2025 (colour of the ground near the field and of the middle ground, blended with the
  scan textures under the ground masks, and the frame every layer is registered to); Cartografia 1:10.000, buildings
  layer (Edificacions), accessed October 2026 (building outlines, rebuilt as house meshes).
- Building heights: Overture Maps Foundation, buildings theme, ODbL 1.0.
  Contains information from OpenStreetMap (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0; Microsoft Global ML Building
  Footprints, ODbL 1.0; work derived from BTN 2024 ign.es (Instituto Geografico Nacional de Espana), CC BY 4.0.
- Roads, paths and water: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
- Terrain: GEDTM30 global ensemble digital terrain model, OpenGeoHub, CC BY 4.0.
- Tree positions and heights: High Resolution Canopy Height Maps by WRI and Meta, CC BY 4.0.
- Colour of the far ground and colour balance of the near ground: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
  2024-2026.
- Ground, rock and bark textures: Poly Haven, CC0.
