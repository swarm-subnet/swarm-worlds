Women and men in twelve builds, from 1.55 to 1.88 m and from slim to heavy, each a shape of the SOMA body with a
skeleton fitted to it and its lips closed. On them: 42 painted skins (seven tones, for dark and for light hair, as a
woman, a man shaved and a man with stubble), eyes in six colours, hair (short, a bob, long, a ponytail, a bun),
tops (T-shirt, tank top, long sleeve, shirt, sweater, jacket, puffer jacket), a safety vest, bottoms (jeans, trousers,
joggers, shorts, leggings, a short and a long skirt), sneakers and boots, a beanie, and a cap and two backpacks carried
over from Sentinel's wardrobe. Clothes and hair are built from the body's own surface, so each piece carries the
body's bone weights and is shaped again on every build; nothing is fitted when a person is dressed.

people.json is the table of contents: the builds with their sex and height, the skins, which eyes go with which
tone, the palettes, and every piece with its slot (eyes, hair, top, bottom, shoes, over, head, back), who wears it,
how warm it is, the bulk of top it is cut for, its temperature for the thermal camera and its pictures, each with the
palette it is tinted from (none for a picture in its own colours). A picture painted pale takes its colour from a
tint.

rig.npz holds the skeleton, each build's fitted bind transforms, rest joints and stride, and the skin's bone weights.
body.npz holds the skin's points on every build (points) and the drawn skin: its triangles (faces), the point each
drawn vertex shows (source) and its place on the skin's picture (uv). pieces/<name>.npz holds a piece: its points on
every build (points), bone weights (weight_joints, weights), drawn triangles (faces, source, uv) and the triangles of
the drawn skin it hides (hides).
