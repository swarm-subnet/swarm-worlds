Five birds, each made from its own measures with no outside model: the carrion crow, the magpie, the wood pigeon,
the town pigeon and the alpine chough. Body, skeleton, the nine motions (walk, hop, stand, look, peck, take off,
fly, glide, land), the coats and the heat maps were all made for this map.

birds.json lists each bird's coats and clips and which clip may follow which. A skin is one surface of shared
vertices: bird_<name>_poses.npz holds every pose of the body's points (poses), the body point each drawn vertex shows
(source), its place on the coat (uv) and the triangles (faces). The OBJ is the same skin at rest, for any tool that
reads one. A bird carries two sets of wings, spread and folded; the set not in use is drawn shrunk inside the body.
