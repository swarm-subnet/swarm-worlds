Source: the dogs of the Test Field, built for this map.
License: CC0 1.0 for the source models; the coats, the heat maps and the reworked motions are our own work.

- Bodies, skeletons and the walking, standing and sniffing motions: the Husky, the Shiba Inu and the Wolf of the
  Ultimate Animated Animal Pack by Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0 1.0. The Wolf carries shepherd coats. Legs were
  given real length, paws planted, and lying down, lying and getting up made for these dogs.
- Coats and heat maps: painted for this map from each dog's own surface.

dogs.json lists the clips and which clip may follow which. Each skin is one surface of shared vertices:
dog_<name>_poses.npz holds every pose of the body's points (poses), the body point each drawn vertex shows (source),
its place on the coat (uv) and the triangles (faces). The OBJ is the same skin at rest, for any tool that reads one.
