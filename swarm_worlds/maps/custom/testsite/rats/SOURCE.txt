Source: the rats and mice of the Test Field, built for this map.
License: our own work.

Two bodies, each made from its own measures with no outside model: the brown rat (body about 0.23 m with a tail nearly
as long, in a brown, a grey and a dark coat) and the mouse (body about 0.09 m with a tail as long, as a wood mouse, a
house mouse and a dark one). Each has bead eyes, round cupped ears and whiskers. Body, skeleton, the eight motions (stand, walk, trot, sniff_walk, sniff, nose_down, look,
groom), the coats and the heat maps were all made for this map. Trot is the scurry, look is rearing up on the hind
legs to scent the air, groom is sitting up to wash the face; neither body lies down.

rats.json lists each body's coats and clips and which clip may follow which. A skin is one surface of shared
vertices: rat_<name>_poses.npz holds every pose of the body's points (poses), the body point each drawn vertex shows
(source), its place on the coat (uv) and the triangles (faces). The OBJ is the same skin at rest, for any tool that
reads one. The tail lies on the ground in every pose.
