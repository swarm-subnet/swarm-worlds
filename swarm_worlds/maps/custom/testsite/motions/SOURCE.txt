Source: the everyday motions of the Test Field's people, generated for this map.
License: our own output of NVIDIA Kimodo (https://github.com/nv-tlabs/kimodo, code under Apache-2.0) with its model
Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2 (NVIDIA Open Model License), on the SOMA skeleton the people stand on (see
../people/LICENSE_SOMA.txt).

91 short blocks at 30 frames a second, 300 seconds in all, each written from a plain sentence ("A person walks
forward at a relaxed normal pace with the arms swinging naturally.") and kept only if it passed its checks: the
frames it is joined on within 3 cm of the pose it joins, a loop closing on itself, feet on the ground sliding under
6 cm a second, no pops. motions.json keeps every block's sentence and what it measured.

What it holds:
- four base poses every block starts or ends on: standing, squatting, kneeling upright on one knee, sitting
  cross-legged;
- ways of walking, each a loop with a block that sets off into it from standing and one that stops out of it: a
  plain walk (two takes), a slow stroll (two takes), a walk while looking at a phone, a casual walk with the arms
  hanging (named walk_pockets); a walker can stop, look up and walk on (walk_look_up, stroll_look_up);
- things done standing, each a loop with its way in and its way back: shifting the weight (idle), talking, looking
  at a phone, arms crossed, hands on the hips, taking a picture, stretching, pointing, looking up at the sky, waving;
- four small moves from standing back to standing, two takes each: a shift of the weight, a look round, a hand to
  the head, a quiet breath;
- low poses: a loop worked in each (touching the ground squatting, looking round kneeling, sitting), and the blocks
  that go down into each pose from standing and get up again.

Everything done on one spot ends exactly where and facing as it began: a small move in itself, a thing done standing
over its way in, its loop and its way back. So a person who stays put never drifts off his place.

The file format is the one of ../../solar/motions (motions.json names it): rotations[first:first + frames] are the
local rotations of 30 joints, root the root's place, every block starting at the origin facing +z, with the rule
for mirroring a block left to right.
