Source: the goat of the Test Field, built for this map.
License: CC0 1.0 for the source model; the reshaping, the coats, the heat map and the motions are our own work.

- Body, skeleton and the walking, grazing and standing motions: the Deer of the Ultimate Animated Animal Pack by
  Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0 1.0, reshaped into a goat (shorter and stouter legs, a deeper barrel, a shorter
  neck, smaller ears, horns and a beard added). Lying down, lying and getting up were made for this goat.
- Coats and heat map: painted for this map from the goat's own surface.

goats.json lists the clips and which clip may follow which. The skin is one surface of shared vertices:
goat_doe_poses.npz holds every pose of the body's points (poses), the body point each drawn vertex shows (source), its
place on the coat (uv) and the triangles (faces). The OBJ is the same skin at rest, for any tool that reads one.
